# Transsion IPO research — checked 7 October 2026

This source and calculation log accompanies the concise analysis. Amounts are RMB unless explicitly labelled HKD or USD. Prices are closing observations, not live quotes.

Revised 8 October 2026 for the H-share-focused trader briefing. Anker is added to the main H table and the closest A references. The revised Word file is published at the repository root as `Transsion_H_IPO_Trader_Brief_2026-10-08.docx`. Price observations remain dated below.

## Calculation conventions

- A-share close: 30 September 2026. H-share closes: 6 October 2026.
- FX used: CNY 1 = HKD 1.1705; USD 1 = HKD 7.848.
- Reported trailing P/E = latest disclosed issued-share count × share price, converted into the profit currency, / profit attributable to owners over the trailing 12 months.
- For December year ends, TTM profit = FY2025 + H1 2026 − H1 2025.
- Lenovo's financial year ends in March; its TTM is to June 2026.
- Figures are approximate. Small changes in treasury shares and share awards can cause differences from terminal P/E based on weighted-average EPS.
- Transsion H valuations apply the offer price to the whole post-issue company, excluding the greenshoe. They are not the value of the H tranche or a mixed A/H market capitalisation.

## 8 October additions — trade sensitivities and Anker

Anker's share count uses 539,356,037 A shares explicitly disclosed in the 18 September next-day share-change report and 50,107,900 H shares after the July IPO and partial greenshoe, disclosed in the H1 report. Total 589,463,937. Later convertible conversions can change the exact count marginally; the rounded one-decimal P/Es are approximate. At the stated prices/FX, the H per-share discount to A is 22.5%.

Anker belongs as an overseas branded consumer-electronics comparator, with chargers, energy storage, eufy and soundcore products. It is not a direct handset competitor. H1 revenue was RMB16.605bn (+29.05%), owner profit RMB1.702bn (+45.86%), and gross margin 49.82%. The H1 report explicitly says refunds of prior-year IEEPA tariffs reduced current cost of sales. Fair-value gains of RMB271.47m also supported reported earnings. Do not interpret all the margin increase as sustainable brand pricing power or automatically apply Anker's multiple to Transsion.

At the Transsion midpoint, the prospectus discloses HKD1,245.50m cornerstone commitments and 33,616,400 shares, 38.80% of the base 86,648,300-share offer. That leaves 53,031,900 non-cornerstone shares in the base offer; it is not a forecast of traded volume or proof of wider book demand. The base offer is initially 10% public / 90% international, subject to reallocation. See printed prospectus pages 210–214 and 276 onward.

Midpoint net proceeds approximately HKD3.118bn: 40% AI R&D, 30% marketing, 20% mobile internet/IoT, 10% working capital. The cash drain is primarily an inventory/working-capital issue, but the whole IPO is not earmarked for memory inventory. H1 foreign-exchange losses were approximately RMB389.664m versus RMB48.120m in H1 2025, another pressure on owner earnings.

Formula: selected owner profit × chosen P/E ÷ 1,237,832,845 post-issue shares × HKD1.1705/RMB. These are illustrative sensitivities, not price targets, probabilities or new broker forecasts. They exclude dividends, greenshoe and future FX changes. The chosen P/E multiples are assumptions.

Desk judgment: the low to midpoint offer provides a credible recovery valuation against the H handset/brand references. Confirmation requires margins on replacement inventory and cash conversion. At the upper offer price, stronger book evidence is needed to compensate for a smaller valuation cushion. A-share discount alone does not establish upside.
