# Four PHIP trader briefs — source and valuation audit

As of 13 October 2026 close. This revision replaces six-target coverage with the four official PHIP issuers. Capchem remains solely a listed Tinci peer; Yuen Kee is removed. The legacy audit filename is retained to update the existing GitHub artifact.

## Valuation consistency

All table multiples are recalculated FY2026E/FY2027E P/E, using selected attributable full-year profit estimates and refreshed 13 October closing market values. These are individual estimates, not consensus. Currency is shown beside each price. Provider TTM P/E is omitted because HK static earnings and A rolling earnings cannot be treated as the same denominator.

A shares: use quoted line-implied total-company equity value, not float cap. The quoted value is rounded by the vendor; we do not manufacture precise share counts by dividing a rounded value by price. HK shares: closing price times total ordinary shares (all classes), not only H shares; convert RMB forecasts at 1 HKD = 0.85753 CNY, the 13 October official central parity. China Travel profit forecasts are HKD, so no conversion. These values are not the combined A+H market cap.

HK final daily bars dated 13 October, retrieved after the market closed, agree with the Tencent snapshot. Sina intraday HK quotes differed and were not used as closing prices. Broker listings were refreshed through 13 October; the dated selected original forecasts below remain the latest verified estimates for those brokers. No claim of a complete market-wide consensus set is made.

Kinwong monthly return confirms 1,002,405,129 A shares + 72,944,300 H shares = 1,075,349,429 total. Its A and H forecast multiples use the same profits and current total share base. FX-adjusted H discount is 37.54%, not the provider A/H premium number.

No issuer offer price, size, greenshoe or named cornerstone terms are final in the four PHIPs. Offer P/E must use RMB-converted H price times post-issue total shares and the same forecast. Microintelligence and Slender West Lake have no verified public FY2026/27 forecasts; latest disclosed LTM earnings are labelled separately. No interim annualisation.

PRC accounts anchor Tinci/Dongshan revenue, margin, owner profit and cash. Their HK drafts are IFRS and have classification differences: Dongshan PRC H1 cash from operations is RMB2.384bn vs RMB2.269bn in PHIP; investing outflow RMB5.483bn vs RMB5.367bn. The brief uses PRC values together, not a mixture.

## Competitive-position and earnings corrections

- Microintelligence: 20.0% is China EIIR niche revenue share, not all industrial automation; broader industrial AI equipment share is 0.3%. Five-month operating profit +1.8%, with redemption interest RMB16.563m to zero. Reported and non-IFRS adjusted LTM profit are separate.
- Tinci: global electrolyte shipment share is 36.0% in FY2025. Integration is a cost advantage, not proof of fixed selling prices. Inventory growth with faster turnover does not establish unsold-stock deterioration; cash conversion/customer credit remains weak.
- Dongshan: global PCB share 4.2%, FPC 24.5%, optics 2.9% FY2025. Optical ranking uses Source Photonics standalone full-year revenue; the group FY2025 consolidated optical mix contains only Q4. Higher H1 mix is real, but not a like-for-like organic growth rate. The rumoured US-content rule is not enacted policy.
- Slender West Lake: Jiangsu 18.5% and national 1.2% are boat-ticket revenue shares. The 18.8% Yangzhou regional-market statistic is a different denominator and is not substituted. Night-show revenue increase (29.489-3.386)m explains 87.7% of FY2025 group revenue increase (141.196-111.428)m. Core boat rights end September 2035; Canal approval/bus rights differ. Owner LTM profit is RMB47.716m, not group profit.

## Trader briefs and evidence

### Microintelligence — PHIP 9 Oct

- **Good - industrial embodied AI with real sales.** No.1 in China's embodied intelligent industrial robots (20% FY2025 share), but only 0.3% of the broader industrial AI equipment market. The attraction is a small specialist becoming a scalable supplier; the ranking does not establish dominance across robotics.
- **Potential - turn installations into repeatable products.** Robots were 61% of five-month 2026 sales, growing 40% at 54.8% gross margin. Agilebot adds motion control to its vision software. Reusing systems across factories could spread heavy R&D costs and lift profit; repeat orders and lower delivery cost are the evidence to watch.
- **Bad - the earnings inflection is weaker than it looks.** Reported five-month profit tripled to RMB22.5m, but operating profit rose only 1.8%; RMB16.6m redemption-interest expense disappeared. Operating cash was just RMB12.7m and collection took 188 days. Customers' factory capex and cash collection still determine whether growth pays.
- **Valuation - keep the AI premium tied to operating profit.** Each RMB1bn of offer equity value means 48.7x reported LTM earnings or 17.1x adjusted LTM earnings, to May 2026. Those are separate bases, neither a forward estimate. Mech-Mind is the closest H-share vision/AI reference; Estun gives a profitable industrial-robot benchmark.

Mech-Mind: closest H vision/AI reference, still loss-making; Estun: industrial robots, with valuation gains in profit. OPT/TZTEK: A-share vision/inspection peers.

### Tinci Materials — PHIP 5 Oct

- **Good - global electrolyte leader, with cycle exposure.** No.1 by FY2025 shipments, with 36% share. Integrated LiPF6/LiFSI production gives a cost advantage. EV/energy-storage demand supports volume; electrolyte and salt prices drive earnings more directly than AI or solid-state themes.
- **Potential - volume and overseas customers can extend recovery.** H1 owner profit reached RMB2.86bn (+968%), with 33.5% gross margin. Yet Q2 margin fell to 29.3% and profit fell 27% QoQ: new competing supply threatens pricing even if shipments grow.
- **Bad - profit has outrun cash.** H1 operating cash was RMB397m, only 14% of owner profit. Inventory rose 59% but turnover improved to 38.6 days, consistent with greater scale. RMB12.15bn receivables/bills make customer credit the more pressing concern.
- **Valuation - low P/E relies on recovered margins.** A shares are 10.7x/8.9x FY2026E/FY2027E; Capchem is 20.8x/15.5x. The FY2026 forecast needs RMB3.38bn H2 profit, above H1. Test sustainable profit per tonne and H-issue dilution before buying the discount.

Capchem/Ruitai: direct electrolyte peers; Do-Fluoride: upstream LiPF6 cycle. No clean H-listed electrolyte comparable.

### Dongshan Precision — PHIP 11 Oct

- **Good - the clearest AI hardware exposure here.** Global No.3 PCB supplier (4.2% FY2025 share) and No.2 FPC (24.5%); Source Photonics is No.7 in optics (2.9%). Established PCB scale, with room for AI optical share gains.
- **Potential - 800G/1.6T modules and AI PCBs can improve earnings mix.** Optics reached 19.2% of H1 revenue; owner profit rose 290% to RMB2.96bn. FY2025 had only Q4 Source Photonics consolidation, so the growth headline includes acquisition effects.
- **Bad - consumer exposure and expansion cash needs remain.** FPC was 36.5% of H1 sales. Operating cash of RMB2.38bn covered less than half of RMB5.48bn investing outflow. AI capex, module yields and export access determine whether the expansion pays.
- **Valuation - the AI ramp is already expected.** A shares are 35.4x/16.8x FY2026E/FY2027E; InnoLight 25.9x/13.9x; Kinwong H 30.0x/17.8x. Dongshan's FY2027 multiple assumes 110% profit growth. Compare the H offer with Kinwong H's lower FY2026 multiple, as well as the A-share discount.

Kinwong/Shennan: PCB; InnoLight: optics. Kinwong H is 37.5% below its A line after FX. Segment references, not whole-group equivalents.

### Slender West Lake Cultural Tourism — PHIP 7 Oct

- **Good - a local concession with strong ticket cash.** No.2 in Jiangsu water tourism (18.5% FY2025 boat-ticket revenue share), versus 1.2% nationally. H1 gross margin was 63.9%; RMB49m operating cash exceeded RMB34.7m owner profit. Local rights support earnings quality.
- **Bad - the growth boost is fading.** H1 revenue and owner profit both fell 1.6%. Night shows supplied 88% of FY2025 revenue growth, but H1 night-show revenue fell 3%. Weather and holidays create seasonality; H1 profit cannot simply be doubled.
- **Potential - higher spending per visitor.** New routes, charters and night shows can add earnings beyond visitor growth. Asset-light management could expand outside Yangzhou, but is only 3.1% of H1 sales. A national-platform valuation needs evidence of profitable replication.
- **Valuation - finite rights limit the premium.** Core boat rights expire September 2035; renewal preference is conditional. Each RMB1bn offer equity value is 21.0x owner LTM earnings to June 2026. Lijiang/Jiuhua are 15-20x FY2026E. A premium requires new growth or credible dividends.

China Travel: H scenic-operator reference, with hotels/transport. A peers match scenic transport more closely; Changbai's multiple is a thematic outlier.
