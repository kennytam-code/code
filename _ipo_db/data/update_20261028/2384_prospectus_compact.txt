OVERVIEW
 We are a manufacturer primarily focused on printed circuit board (“PCBs”), precision components,
touch panels and liquid crystal modules (“LCMs”) and optical transceivers, with a global presence and
operating footprint. Our products are widely used in consumer electronics, automobiles, data centers,
telecommunications equipment and industrial control devices, ultimately enabling the connection of people,
devices and infrastructure. In September 2025, we entered the optical transceiver business through our
acquisition of Source Photonics, thereby expanding our product portfolio to include optical transceivers.
 According to China Insights Consultancy (“CIC”) and by revenue in 2025, we are ranked as the
third-largest PCB supplier in the US$85.2 billion global PCB market, and we ranked seventh amongst
global optical transceiver suppliers with a market share of 2.9% of the US$24.5 billion global optical
transceiver market based on the revenue of Source Photonics in 2025. Our PCB, precision component,
touch panel and LCM products serve the consumer electronics, automotive and telecommunications end
markets. In parallel, our optical transceiver and AI PCB products targeting the data center end market
represent our primary growth drivers, with the demand driven by the accelerating global build-out of
AI-related infrastructure.
 We are a long-standing partner to four of the world’s top five consumer electronics brands, as well as
the top five electric vehicle (“EV”) manufacturers and the top five cloud service providers. Our close
customer collaboration, intelligent manufacturing capabilities and global footprint have proven critical to
meeting our customers’ stringent requirements to ensure product quality, delivery efficiency and supply
chain resilience.

Our Products
 Our products include PCBs, precision components, touch panels and LCMs and optical transceivers.
We provide distinctive and comprehensive solutions by leveraging technological innovation and advanced
manufacturing and creating synergies across product, R&D, technology, supply chain and sales. The
following highlights our main products and solutions and their respective applications.

 SUMMARY

 • PCBs: Our PCB products include flexible printed circuit (“FPC”), rigid printed circuit board
 (“RPCB”) and Rigid-Flex for consumer electronics, automotive, data centers and AI servers and
 telecommunications equipment. We serve leading global consumer electronics manufacturers,
 automakers and cloud service providers. Through these partnerships, we have built deep technical
 and process capabilities that reinforce our competitive advantage.
 • Precision Components: Our precision components primarily include automotive components and
 telecommunications equipment components. We have strong capabilities in precision components
 for the automotive and telecommunications industries. Over the past decade, we have extended
 our R&D and manufacturing capabilities developed in telecommunications components into
 automotive, broadening downstream applications and establishing long-term partnerships with
 major automakers worldwide and laying a solid foundation for further growth.
 • Touch Panels and LCMs: We offer touch panels and liquid crystal display (“LCD”) and organic
 lightemitting diode (“OLED”) modules used in automobiles, smartphones, tablets, laptops,
 industrial control devices and smart home applications. Building on years of experience in
 consumer touch displays, we expanded into in-vehicle displays in 2023, where our proven
 technology and commercial execution continue to provide a strong platform for growth.
 • Optical Transceivers: In September 2025, we entered the optical transceiver business through our
 acquisition of Source Photonics, a global provider of optical transceivers. Through the acquisition,
 we have gained in-house optical chip capabilities centered on high-speed electro-absorption
 modulated laser (“EML”) chips with transmission rates from 2.5G to 200G. We currently focus
 on commercializing high-end 100G and 200G PAM4 EML chips, while advancing the
 development of next-generation technologies such as 400G EML chips and continuous
 wave-based (“CW-based”) silicon photonics solution. In addition, we offer optical transceivers
 from 10G to 1.6T and are developing next-generation transceivers of 3.2T or higher.

Our Strategic M&A
 In parallel with our organic growth, we pursue strategic investments and acquisitions from time to time
to broaden our product portfolio, enhance our core technologies and expand our global reach. In particular,
we completed the following acquisitions in the second half of 2025:
 • Source Photonics. Source Photonics is an optical transceiver supplier that operates principally
 through its key R&D base in China and its manufacturing facilities in China and Thailand.
 Following the completion of our acquisition of a 97.48% equity interest in Source Photonics in
 September 2025, optical transceivers became a new business segment of our Group. This segment
 accounted for 3.6% and 19.2% of our revenue in 2025 and the six months ended June 30, 2026,
 respectively. It recorded gross profit margins of 36.8% and 39.2% for the same periods,
 supporting our overall profitability. The acquisition strengthened our capabilities in optical
 transceivers as well as the broader AI infrastructure markets.
 • Groupe Mécanique Découpage (“ GMD”). GMD is a precision component supplier principally
 engaged in the manufacture and sale of automotive components in Europe. We acquired 100% of
 GMD in October 2025. Following the acquisition, the sales volume of our automotive
 components increased from 79.4 million units in the six months ended June 30, 2025 to
 161.3 million units in the six months ended June 30, 2026. Over the same periods, their average
 selling price increased from RMB25.7 to RMB38.6 per unit. These increases drove the revenue
 contribution of our automotive components from 12.0% to 22.4% of our total revenue over the
 same periods. The acquisition enhanced our precision component capabilities and extended our
 operating footprint into Europe, enabling us to better serve international automotive and industrial
 customers.
 These transactions demonstrate our track record of executing strategic investments, integrating
operations and supply chains, and realizing commercial and technological synergies across our global
platform.

RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT
 We consistently invest in research and development to expand our product portfolio, seize new market
opportunities and strengthen our competitive edge in meeting the demands of leading global brands.
 Due to the customized nature of our products, our R&D is often carried out in close collaboration with
customers based on their customization requirements and end product designs. We have enhanced our R&D
efforts by advancing new materials, design innovations and process technologies. These advancements have
resulted in a series of significant breakthroughs, earning us the trust of leading global companies as a
reliable and innovative supplier.
 As of June 30, 2026, our R&D personnel accounted for approximately 11.3% of our total workforce,
placing us in a competitive position in the global PCB industry and reflecting our strong focus on
innovation. We continuously digitalize and automate our facilities to push the limits of advanced processes
in intelligent manufacturing and achieve organic growth.

Our Customers
 Our customers are mainly global brands in the consumer electronics, telecommunications and automotive
