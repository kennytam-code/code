Subject: TSMC 3Q26 preview | 15 October: broker beat/miss calls, channel evidence and earnings reactions

Team,

TSMC reports 3Q26 on Thursday, 15 October; the call is at 14:00 Hong Kong / Taipei (02:00 ET). The revenue beat is largely visible already: published monthly sales total NT$1.494tn, or roughly US$46.7bn at the guidance FX assumption. Our read is that the share-price reaction will depend most on Q4 growth and margins, N2 ramp costs, and 2027 capacity / pricing visibility. Call calendar (https://investor.tsmc.com/english/financial-calendar) / monthly sales (https://investor.tsmc.com/english/monthly-revenue/2026).

Quick take: Citi explicitly expects a beat to company guidance; Goldman’s model implies revenue and gross margin above guidance; Wedbush expects Q3 margin upside to its own model but sees Q4 margin below consensus. JPMorgan and Macquarie sit near the Q3 margin guide ceiling. The public EPS whisper is US$4.58 per ADR versus US$4.45 consensus. The table below identifies the metric and benchmark behind each beat / miss view.

5. What the market wants to hear this time — our interpretation

Focus | What would support a positive read | What could disappoint |

Q4 revenue guide | Sustained sequential growth after the known Q3 beat. JPM / Goldman’s earlier USD models are around +11% QoQ; focus on the guide’s midpoint against the final Q3 USD base. | A cautious midpoint, or bottlenecks preventing AI demand from converting into shipments. Wedbush’s +7% NTD model highlights a lower-growth case; FX prevents a direct USD comparison. |

Q4 gross margin / N2 costs | Mid-66% or better would clear the 66.1% consensus reference cited by Wedbush; ~67% would approach Goldman’s stronger case. Evidence of better N2 utilisation / pricing matters. | ~65% GM, or N2 / overseas dilution persisting longer than bullish forecasts. The company previously flagged 3–4 percentage points of H2 N2 dilution. |

2027 AI, pricing and capacity | Clear demand visibility across GPUs, ASICs and server CPUs; confidence in N3 / N2 and CoWoS expansion, and pricing sufficient to fund the ramp. | Customer delays, supply constraints worsening, or much higher investment without corresponding revenue / returns visibility. |

Capex / earnings quality | Directional support for the large 2027 capacity step in broker models, with disciplined execution; recurring earnings backing the headline EPS beat. | Costs outrunning utilisation, or profit strength relying heavily on non-operating gains. A firm 2027 capex number may wait until the January call. |

Suggested framing: “The Q3 sales beat is already known; the incremental test is whether Q4 margins and the 2027 capacity / pricing narrative can justify another round of estimate upgrades. The public EPS whisper is only 3% above consensus, but stronger broker margin assumptions set a higher bar for the outlook.”
