## Sources and interpretation

Company guidance and monthly revenue are primary data. Consensus EPS and the EPS whisper use the same provider, Earnings Whispers. Net profit uses LSEG SmartEstimate as reported by Reuters; this is accuracy-weighted and is a different analyst basket. No revenue / margin / profit whisper has been invented. Most broker figures are public news summaries, not original client reports. The UBS row is explicitly identified as a secondary interpretation, with lower confidence.

The broker table is organised around **beat / miss stance, metric and benchmark, supporting operating / channel evidence, and outlook risk**. “Explicit” means the source states an upside call. “Model-implied” means the author's arithmetic comparison with company guidance; it does not imply an explicit Street EPS beat call. Goldman implies above-guide revenue / GM, JPM and Macquarie are at or near the GM guide ceiling, and Wedbush explicitly sees Q3 GM upside to its own model while modelling Q4 GM below consensus. Morgan Stanley's cited preview provides no quarterly beat / miss call.

The proposed market hurdles are editorial interpretation of the cited public previews, not measured buy-side consensus. Earlier broker models may predate the September revenue release. Missing numbers are disclosed as unavailable. There is no claim that first-hand supplier interviews were conducted for this email. Reported broker operating assessments, third-party industry research and actual supplier announcements are identified separately. Price reactions use the U.S. ADR.

## Arithmetic and event-window definitions

- Q3 NTD revenue = 467.580 + 514.806 + 511.857 = **NT$1,494.243bn**.
- Indicative USD revenue = NTD revenue / 32 = **US$46.695bn**, **1.95% above** the US$45.8bn guidance ceiling. This is an assumed-FX conversion, not reported final USD revenue.
- EPS whisper premium = 4.58 / 4.45 − 1 = **2.92%**.
- Wedbush Q4 NTD revenue growth = 1,599 / 1,494.243 − 1 = **7.01%**; it cannot be compared directly with USD growth without FX assumptions.
- Profit beat = actual / contemporaneous LSEG SmartEstimate − 1, using Reuters's rounded figures.
- T = the U.S. reporting-day session. Prior five sessions = T−6 close to T−1 close; day reaction = T−1 to T; first five reaction sessions = T−1 to T+4, so this window includes the reporting day.
- All returns use NYSE ADR unadjusted closing prices and exclude dividends. Broader market moves are not removed. January's window skips the Martin Luther King Jr. holiday.
- Four quarters is a small descriptive sample. Q2 2026 includes NT$63bn of Vanguard gains, as stated in TSMC's transcript.
