## Why this CPI is different

*The Fed setup*

1. July's FOMC held at 3.50-3.75% by 9-3, and all three dissents were for a HIKE. A three-dissent hold is a committee sitting one print away from moving.
2. Warsh told Jackson Hole on 28 August that clear evidence of cooling has not appeared and that underlying inflation has not meaningfully improved.
3. The inflation is supply-driven, from the energy shock tied to the Iran conflict. A central bank normally looks through that. This one cannot, because the argument has shifted from demand to credibility.
4. Public pricing venues genuinely disagree. One read of CME FedWatch has a 66% hike, another summary still shows a cut favoured, and Kalshi and Polymarket had a hold near 70% with a hike near 28% in mid-August.
5. When the venues disagree by that much, the data does the deciding rather than the guidance. That is what makes Friday 20:30 HKT the whole week.

> **Verdict.** Trade the dispersion, not the direction. The honest position into Friday is optionality rather than a Fed view, and Monday 14 September in Asia is the session that gaps on the answer. Pull live pricing off WIRP before this goes out - do not send a public probability.

## Broadcom just moved the bar, and the constraint list is the tell

*Context - reported Wednesday 2 September*

| Line | Q3 FY26 | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $29.6bn, +86% y/y | beat |
| AI semiconductors | $16.7bn, +221% y/y, +54% q/q | more than tripled |
| Adjusted EPS | $3.32 | beat |
| Q4 revenue guide | $34.8bn vs $35.03bn consensus | MISSED the total |
| FY26 AI revenue | $58bn | guided |
| FY27 / FY28 AI outlook | ~$115bn / ~$230bn | the number the multiple hangs on |
| Named customers | Google, Anthropic, OpenAI, Meta | custom silicon, not merchant GPUs |
| Stated constraints | power, land, advanced wafers, substrates, HBM | physical, not demand |

1. The total revenue guide missed while AI tripled. The market is paying for mix now, not for the revenue line.
2. Every constraint management named is physical build-out. Demand was not on the list.
3. That is an Asia sentence, not a US one. If the bottleneck is wafers, substrates, memory and power, the margin sits with TSMC on CoWoS, SK Hynix and Samsung on HBM, Ibiden and Unimicron on substrates, and Delta and LiteOn on power.

> **Verdict.** Own the constraint, not the customer. The names that clear are the ones selling into a shortage they cannot be designed out of within a year.

## Apple, and what the foldable actually changes

*Asia supply chain*

- Keynote 01:00 HKT Thursday, which means Asia trades the read before New York opens.
- Expected: iPhone 18 Pro, 18 Pro Max, and a foldable positioned as iPhone Ultra. The base iPhone 18 slips to spring 2027.
- The split cadence is the structural news. September stops being the whole year for the assemblers, and part of the revenue moves into H1 2027.
- A foldable at Ultra pricing lifts bill-of-materials content per unit far more than it lifts units.

> **Verdict.** Buy content, not volume. Hinge, display and optics content gains; assembly volume does not. And do not extrapolate a September build number to the full year this time - the calendar changed underneath the model.
