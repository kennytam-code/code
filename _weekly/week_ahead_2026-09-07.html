The Fed setup
Why this CPI is different
1.July's FOMC held at 3.50–3.75% by nine votes to three, and all three dissents were for a hike. A three-dissent hold is a committee sitting one print away from moving.
2.Warsh told Jackson Hole on 28 August that clear evidence of cooling has not appeared and that underlying inflation has not meaningfully improved.
3.The inflation is supply-driven, out of the energy shock tied to the Iran conflict. A central bank normally looks straight through that. This one cannot, because the argument has moved from demand to credibility.
4.Public pricing venues genuinely disagree. One read of CME FedWatch has a 66% hike, another summary still shows a cut favoured, and Kalshi and Polymarket had a hold near 70% with a hike near 28% in mid-August.
5.When the venues disagree by that much, the data does the deciding rather than the guidance. Which is exactly what makes Friday at 20:30 HKT the whole week.
Verdict
Trade the dispersion, not the direction. The honest position into Friday is optionality rather than a Fed view, and Monday 14 September in Asia is the session that gaps on the answer. Pull live pricing off the terminal before this circulates: none of the public numbers above belong in a sent email.

Stated constraintspower, land, wafers, substrates, HBMphysical, not demand
1.The total revenue guide missed while AI revenue tripled. The market is paying for mix now, not for the revenue line.
2.Every constraint management named is physical build-out. Demand did not appear on the list at all.
3.That is an Asia sentence, not a US one. If the bottleneck is wafers, substrates, memory and power, the margin sits with TSMC on advanced packaging, SK Hynix and Samsung on high-bandwidth memory, Ibiden and Unimicron on substrates, and Delta and LiteOn on power.
Verdict
Own the constraint, not the customer. The names that clear from here are the ones selling into a shortage that cannot be designed out inside a year.
Thursday's tech triple
Oracle, Adobe, and Jensen on a stage
•Oracle FQ1 FY27, 05:00 HKT Friday. Remaining performance obligation is the only number that matters. Last September's backlog print is what re-rated the stock, so the comparison is unforgiving. Watch cloud growth against capex and debt, and watch the gap between booked backlog and cash conversion.
•Adobe FQ3, same slot. Net-new Digital Media annual recurring revenue is the line. This is the cleanest listed test of whether generative AI compounds an incumbent's moat or dissolves it, and the stock has been trading as though the answer is already settled.
•Nvidia at Goldman Communacopia, 23:50 HKT Thursday. Any remark on merchant GPU share against custom silicon moves the entire complex, and it moves the Asia supply chain overnight rather than at the New York open.
Verdict
Three catalysts, one theme. The AI trade is being repriced from demand to delivery. Position for the rotation inside the sector, not for beats.
Asia supply chain
Apple, and what a foldable actually changes
•Keynote at 01:00 HKT Thursday, which means Asia trades the read before New York opens.
•Expected: iPhone 18 Pro, 18 Pro Max, and a foldable positioned as iPhone Ultra. The base iPhone 18 slips to spring 2027.
•The split cadence is the real structural news. September stops being the whole year for the assemblers, and a chunk of revenue moves into the first half of 2027.
•A foldable at Ultra pricing lifts bill-of-materials content per unit far more than it lifts units.
Verdict
Buy content, not volume. Hinge, display and optics content gains; assembly volume does not. And do not extrapolate a September build number to the full year this time, because the calendar changed underneath the model.
